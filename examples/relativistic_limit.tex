% Low-momentum limit of the relativistic energy of a free particle.
For a free particle of mass $m$, with $m, c > 0$, the relativistic energy is
\begin{align}
  E &= \sqrt{p^2 c^2 + m^2 c^4} \\
    &= m c^2 \sqrt{1 + \frac{p^2}{m^2 c^2}} .
\end{align}
Expanding the square root for small $p$, to second order in $p$, gives the rest energy
plus the Newtonian kinetic energy,
\begin{equation}
  E \approx m c^2 + \frac{p^2}{2m} .
\end{equation}
